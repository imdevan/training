## Be consistent

Use the same markers everytime. It is boring for you, but makes it clear to the dog what you are asking for.

### Keep treats on you on walks

Add random cues at random intervals on your walks. This keeps them on their toes!

## Behavior Training Flow Chart

```mermaid reward-flow-chart
flowchart TD
    A((Give cue, wait a few seconds for dog to process)) --> B{Did they do the thing?}
    B -->|yes| By@{ shape: dbl-circ, label: "Reward! 🎉" }
    B -->|no| C{Are you training or having fun?}
    C -->|fun| Cn[Cute Correction]
    C -->|training| Cy[Neutral Correction]
    Cn --> D[Wait a few seconds to reset]
    Cy --> D
    D --> E[Give cue a second time, no change in tone or anything]
    E --> F{Did they do the thing?}
    F -->|yes| Fy@{ shape: dbl-circ, label: "Reward! 🎉" }
    F -->|no| G[Simplify]
    G --> A
```
