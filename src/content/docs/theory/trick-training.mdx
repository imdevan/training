## Be consistent

Use the same markers everytime. It is boring for you, but makes it clear to the dog what you are asking for.

### Keep treats on you on walks

Add random cues at random intervals on your walks. This keeps them on their toes!

## Reward flow chart

```mermaid
flowchart TD
    A[Did they do what you asked?] -->|yes| B["Yes / Good cue + treat"]
    A -->|no| C{Did they do something cute?}
    C -->|no| D["Neutral reward: say 'no' once, neutral tone, no reward<br/>OR wait a few seconds, walk away, come back, retry cue once (neutral tone)"]
    D --> E{Did they do what you asked?}
    E -->|yes| H["Yes/Good cue + treat"]
    E -->|no| G[Reduce the complexity of the ask]
    C -->|yes| I["Ideally no reward."]
    I --> J["If you really want to, give verbal praise (not a Positive Marker) — still rewarding, but less, and not 'telling' them they did what you asked"]
```
