## Be consistent

Use the same markers everytime. It is boring for you, but makes it clear to the dog what you are asking for.

### Keep treats on you on walks

Add random cues at random intervals on your walks. This keeps them on their toes!

## Reward flow chart

  did they do something you thought was cute?
  - no:
    - neutral reward. you may say "no" in a neutral tone once with no reward.
    - otherwise do not do anything. wait a few seconds. walk away, come back, ask again for the aciton
      - try the cue again. only, once, in a neutral tone.
